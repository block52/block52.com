The protocol

Poker as a protocol.

Block52 is not a poker site with a blockchain bolted on. It is an application-specific chain built on the Cosmos SDK and written in Go, with a validator model drawn from THORChain: independent operators bond stake to run the network, and the same validators sign every USDC withdrawal back to Ethereum. The poker engine runs inside every validator as a deterministic state machine. A product of Nine High Studios.

consensus
CometBFT v0.38: Byzantine fault-tolerant, instant finality. A hand is final the block it lands in.
framework
Cosmos SDK v0.53 with a custom x/poker module for tables, actions, payouts and the bridge.
language
Go 1.25, chain and engine alike. One language from the felt to the state root.
engine
Embedded in-process, pinned by Go module version. Every validator executes every action and must reach the same bytes.
validators
Bonded, independent operators. Consensus-breaking releases ship through a coordinated halt so every node switches on the same block.
settlement
USDC, bridged from Ethereum mainnet. Withdrawals are validator-signed and redeemed on Ethereum.
clock
Block time, never wall time. Timeouts are a permissionless action, and the chain sweeps stalled turns itself in EndBlock.
10,000 / 10,000

Real hands from the Pluribus dataset replayed through the engine, every finishing stack reproduced to the chip.

10.5M calls

Engine actions recorded and replayed old-against-new before the latest engine release. 0 regressions.

1 clock

Block execution reads the block's time, never a validator's own time.Now(). Same input, same block, same bytes on every validator.

How it works

From deposit to river, nothing off the books.

The game engine runs inside the chain itself. Validators agree on every action the same way a blockchain agrees on a payment.

Deposit

Send USDC to the bridge. The same amount is credited to your Block52 address on chain.

Sit down

Pick a cash table or a Sit & Go. Your buy-in moves from your balance to the table, on chain.

Play

Each bet, call, check and fold is a signed transaction. The engine on every validator applies it and agrees on the result.

Cash out

Leave the table and withdraw. A validator signs your withdrawal and the bridge pays out USDC on Ethereum.

Why on chain

Trust the record, not the room.

Online poker usually asks you to take the operator's word for it. On Block52 the record is public and anyone can check it.

🃏

Shuffles from the chain

Each deck is seeded from on-chain randomness the validators agree on, not from a server you can't see.

🔁

Replay any hand

Every finished hand opens read-only at its showdown. Share it with one link: ?hand=N

🔎

Open explorer

Blocks, transactions, accounts and hand history are public. Look up any player or table.

Chips are USDC held at your own address. Your balance is chain state, readable by anyone.

🏆

Automatic payouts

Sit & Go places and prizes are worked out by the engine and recorded on chain. Finishers claim their prize in one click.

🖼️
