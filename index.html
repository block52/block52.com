How it works

From deposit to river, nothing off the books.

The game engine runs inside the chain itself. Validators agree on every action the same way a blockchain agrees on a payment.

Deposit

Send USDC to the bridge. The same amount is credited to your Block52 address on chain.

Sit down

Pick a cash table or a Sit & Go. Your buy-in moves from your balance to the table, on chain.

Play

Each bet, call, check and fold is a signed transaction. The engine on every validator applies it and agrees on the result.

Cash out

Leave the table and withdraw. A validator signs your withdrawal and the bridge pays out USDC on Ethereum.

Why on chain

Trust the record, not the room.

Online poker usually asks you to take the operator's word for it. On Block52 the record is public and anyone can check it.

🃏

Shuffles from the chain

Each deck is seeded from on-chain randomness the validators agree on, not from a server you can't see.

🔁

Replay any hand

Every finished hand opens read-only at its showdown. Share it with one link: ?hand=N

🔎

Open explorer

Blocks, transactions, accounts and hand history are public. Look up any player or table.

💵

Your balance, on chain

Chips are USDC held at your own address. Your balance is chain state, readable by anyone.

🏆

Automatic payouts

Sit & Go places and prizes are worked out by the engine and recorded on chain. Finishers claim their prize in one click.

🖼️

Bring your NFT

Link an NFT you own and it shows as your avatar at the table.
